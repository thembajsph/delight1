SECTION 2: COMPLETE FEE STRUCTURE (your revenue model)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Every single fee, who pays it, and when — fully transparent.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A. DELIGHT PLATFORM FEES (your revenue)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Transaction Type              Fee     Who Pays    When Charged
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Person → Person (P2P)         1.5%    Sender      On each send
  Consumer → Business           2.0%    Consumer    On each payment
  Business → Business (B2B)     2.0%    Sender      On each payment
  Ticket Purchase               3.0%    Buyer       On ticket sale
  Ticket Resale (royalty)       3.0%    Seller      On resale (NFT royalty)

  These fees are deducted automatically from the transaction amount
  and credited to the Delight platform XRP wallet.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
B. TOP-UP FEES (ZAR → XRP)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  EXAMPLE: User tops up R200

  Step                          Amount      Who takes it
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  User pays ZAR                 R200.00
  Ozow EFT fee (1.5%)          - R3.00     Ozow
  VALR exchange fee (0.1%)     - R0.20     VALR
  XRP delivered to wallet       196.80 ÷ R9.50/XRP = 20.71 XRP

  Using MoonPay instead:
  MoonPay fee (2–4%)           - R6.00     MoonPay
  XRP delivered to wallet       194.00 ÷ R9.50/XRP = 20.42 XRP

  Note: You can optionally add your own spread on top of this.
  Example: Buy at R9.50, sell to user at R9.75 = R0.25 margin per XRP.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
C. CASH OUT FEES (XRP → ZAR)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  EXAMPLE: User cashes out 20 XRP at R9.50/XRP = R190 gross

  Step                          Amount      Who takes it
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  XRP sold on VALR              20 XRP
  VALR sell fee (0.1%)         - R0.19     VALR
  PayShap transfer              R0          FREE via PayShap
  User receives                 R189.81

  Optional: Add Delight cash-out fee (e.g. 0.5%) = R0.95 per R190

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
D. XRPL NETWORK FEE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Every transaction on the XRP Ledger costs 12 drops = 0.000012 XRP
  At R9.50/XRP this is R0.000114 — effectively zero.
  This is paid by the sender automatically.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
E. FULL EXAMPLE: USER JOURNEY WITH ALL FEES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  1. User A (consumer) tops up R500 via Ozow
     - Ozow fee: R7.50
     - VALR fee: R0.49
     - XRP credited: R492.01 ÷ R9.50 = 51.79 XRP

  2. User A pays a street vendor R100 worth of XRP (10.53 XRP)
     - Delight fee (Consumer→Business 2%): 0.21 XRP (R2.00)
     - Vendor receives: 10.32 XRP (R98.00)
     - Delight earns: R2.00

  3. User A buys event ticket (25 XRP = R237.50)
     - Delight fee (Ticket 3%): 0.75 XRP (R7.13)
     - Organizer receives: 24.25 XRP (R230.38)
     - Delight earns: R7.13

  4. User A sends 5 XRP to friend (P2P)
     - Delight fee (P2P 1.5%): 0.075 XRP (R0.71)
     - Friend receives: 4.925 XRP (R46.79)
     - Delight earns: R0.71

  Total Delight revenue from this one user journey: R9.84
  All on-chain, transparent, real-time.

F. WHAT THE FEES LOOK LIKE IN THE APP (B2B / P2P labels)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  In the app the fees are shown clearly on the send screen:

    Amount you send:      10.00 XRP
    Platform fee (1.5%):   0.15 XRP   ← this is "P2P" shown to user
    Total deducted:       10.15 XRP
    Recipient receives:   10.00 XRP

  For business payments (shown as "Business Payment"):
    Amount:               10.00 XRP
    Platform fee (2.0%):   0.20 XRP   ← shown as "Consumer→Business"
    Total deducted:       10.20 XRP
    Recipient receives:   10.00 XRP

  Ticket purchases (shown on event screen):
    Ticket price:         25.00 XRP
    Platform fee (3.0%):   0.75 XRP   ← shown as "Ticket fee"
    Total deducted:       25.75 XRP
    Organizer receives:   24.25 XRP

  How it works with Delight:
  1. User dials their bank's USSD code
  2. Navigates to "Pay" → "PayShap" or "EFT"
  3. Enters Delight's ShapID (register at your business bank)
  4. Enters the reference number shown in the app
  5. Approves — money leaves their account instantly
  6. Stitch/Ozow webhook fires → backend buys XRP → credits wallet
  7. User gets push notification: "R200 → 21.0 XRP added"
